Private note no. 01Written for Unnamed

Dear Unnamed, with all my nerve

To the one who makes my quiet days feel full,

I’m not the loudest when it comes to feelings, but I just want to say this: I really like you. Not only when you’re around other people, but also when you’re simply being yourself. 😺

You’re loud, weird, unpredictable in the best way, and always genuine. Honestly, that’s what makes you stand out. That’s what made me like you.

It’s not just entertaining. It’s comforting. Hearing you yap, even when I barely say anything, fills the silence in a way that makes it feel warm instead of empty. I could listen to you for hours and still want to hear more. I hope I get to hear it for a long time. And if I’m lucky, maybe even forever. 🐾

I’ve noticed the way you care. The way you check in, listen without judgment, and try to understand people. Even if that’s simply the kind of person you are with everyone, it still means something special to me.

I want you to know that I don’t take any of it for granted. Your time, your energy, and your choice to call me every day, even when I’m quiet, mean more to me than you probably realize. I’m sorry if I can’t always give the same energy back. I may not always know how to show it, but I promise I’ll do my best to give you the same care and effort you give me. 😸

I’m not writing this to make anything weird or to pressure you into anything. I’m writing this because I think you deserve to know how deeply you’re appreciated, especially by me.

You’ve made space for me in ways I didn’t even know I needed. 🐱

I want to know more about you. The good, the bad, the loud, the weird. All of it.

I want to be there for the highs and the lows. Not just for the fun parts, but also for the messy ones, the parts that might make other people walk away. I want to understand what keeps you going, what weighs on you, what makes you happy, and what makes you feel most like yourself. 😻

Just know that you are the warmest color in my mind. 💖

If you ever feel the same way about me, I’ll be here, without hesitation.

And even if you don’t, I’ll still be grateful that I got the chance to know this version of you. The version who laughs loudly, talks nonstop, says the weirdest things, and somehow makes the silence feel like home. 😻

— Icon ₊˚.
